The Vessel

Meet the Finaddict

A 41-ft custom Radon Sportfisher, rebuilt stem to stern in 2016. Locally built in Santa Barbara and renowned for strength, stability, and a comfortable ride.

  • Flying bridge with integrated swim step
  • Spacious cockpit — plenty of room for 6 passengers
  • Full galley with microwave, refrigerator, coffee maker & BBQ
  • Private restroom/shower with hot water
  • Cabin bunks for overnight trips
  • Raymarine electronics — depth sounder, chart plotter, 48-mile radar
  • Annual voluntary Coast Guard inspection
