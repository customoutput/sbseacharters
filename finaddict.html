About the Vessel

Built for the Santa Barbara Channel

Finaddict is a 41-ft custom built Radon Sportfisher with a flying bridge and integrated swim step. Radon boats are locally built in Santa Barbara and are renowned for their strength, stability, and comfortable ride.

Custom built and designed for offshore sportfishing and specialty trips, Finaddict was completely rebuilt from bow to stern in 2016 — making her the newest, largest, and most well-appointed charter boat in the Santa Barbara Harbor.

  • Sleeps up to 6 passengers and 3 crew comfortably
  • Hot and cold running fresh water throughout
  • Private restroom/shower enclosed outside the cabin
  • Equipped with a water maker for longer trips
  • Flush deck and 1¼″ stainless steel rails for safety
  • Flood lights illuminate entire deck for night trips
  • Galley with microwave, refrigerator, and coffee maker
  • Outdoor BBQ grill for multi-day trips

Technical Specifications

Complete Vessel Data

▸ Vessel Specifications — M/V Finaddict
Hull
41-ft custom Radon Sportfisher — locally built in Santa Barbara, CA. Completely rebuilt bow to stern in 2016.
Propulsion
Twin 435 horsepower Volvo Penta D6-435 diesel engines — new, reliable offshore performance.
Speed & Range
Cruising speed up to 20 knots. Operating range of 400 miles.
Generator
12.5 kW onboard generator providing 110V and 220V throughout the vessel. Charge all electrical devices underway.
▸ Engine room — add photo: finaddict-engine.jpg
Water Systems
Hot and cold running fresh water. Equipped with an onboard water maker for extended offshore trips.
Capacity
Up to 6 passengers and 3 crew. Large cockpit with plenty of room to spare. 100% private charter — your group only.
Fishing Systems
1-ton insulated fish hold below deck. 10-scoop live bait tank. Large cockpit with transom door for swim step access. Built-in ice chest supplementing the galley refrigerator.
▸ Cockpit fishing setup — add photo: finaddict-fishing.jpg
Electronics
Full Raymarine suite: depth sounder, chart plotter, 48-mile radar, autopilot — duplicated on the bridge. Second GPS and chart plotter. Two VHF marine radios. 406 MHz Category 1 EPIRB.
Safety
Annual voluntary U.S. Coast Guard inspection. Fire and safety equipment exceeds minimum requirements. Flush deck with 1¼″ stainless steel rails. Flood lighting for full deck coverage on night trips.
Head & Facilities
Private restroom/shower enclosed outside the main cabin. Hot water shower and flushing toilet.
Cabin
Bunks for 6 passengers. Two dining/work tables. Galley with microwave, refrigerator, fresh water sink, and built-in coffee maker. Separate red and white cabin lighting for night use.
Deck & Cockpit
Large cockpit accommodating 6 passengers comfortably. Transom door for easy swim step access. Outdoor BBQ grill for multi-day trips.
