About the Vessel

Built for the Santa Barbara Channel

Finaddict is a 41-ft long and 12.5-ft wide custom built Radon Sportfisher with a flying bridge and integrated swim step. Radon boats are locally built in Santa Barbara and are renowned for their strength, stability, and comfortable ride.

Custom built and designed for offshore sportfishing and specialty trips, Finaddict was completely rebuilt from bow to stern in 2016 — making her the newest, largest, and most well-appointed charter boat in the Santa Barbara Harbor.

  • Sleeps up to 6 passengers and 3 crew comfortably
  • Hot and cold running fresh water throughout
  • Private restroom/shower enclosed outside the cabin
  • Flush deck and 1¼″ stainless steel rails for safety
  • Flood lights illuminate entire deck for night trips
  • Galley with microwave, sink and refrigerator
Technical Specifications

Complete Vessel Data

▸ Vessel Specifications — Finaddict
Hull
41-ft custom Radon Sportfisher — locally built in Santa Barbara, CA. Completely rebuilt bow to stern in 2016.
Propulsion
Twin Volvo Penta D6-435 diesel engines.
Speed & Range
Cruising speed up to 20 knots. Operating range of 400 miles.
Generator
12.5 kW onboard generator providing 110V and 220V throughout the vessel. Charge all electrical devices underway.
Water Systems
Hot and cold running fresh water.
Capacity
Up to 6 passengers and 3 crew. Large cockpit with plenty of room to spare.
Fishing Systems
1-ton insulated fish hold below deck. 8-scoop live bait tank. Large cockpit with transom door for swim step access. Built-in ice chest supplementing the galley refrigerator.
Electronics
Full Raymarine suite: depth sounder, chart plotter, 48-mile radar, autopilot — duplicated on the bridge. Second GPS and chart plotter. Two VHF marine radios. 406 MHz Category 1 EPIRB.
Safety
Annual voluntary U.S. Coast Guard inspection. Fire and safety equipment exceeds minimum requirements. Flush deck with 1¼″ stainless steel rails. Flood lighting for full deck coverage on night trips.
Head & Facilities
Private restroom/shower enclosed outside the main cabin. Hot water shower and marine toilet.
Cabin
Bunks for 6 passengers. Two dining/work tables. Galley with microwave, refrigerator and fresh water sink. Separate red and white cabin lighting for night use.
Deck & Cockpit
Large cockpit accommodating 6 passengers comfortably. Transom door for easy swim step access.

Sportfishing, whale watching, and coastal adventures aboard the Finaddict. Departing from Santa Barbara Harbor.
